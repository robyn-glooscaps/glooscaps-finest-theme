## Usage rules

- Preserve source pixels; do not redraw, recolour, crop the emblem, add effects, or alter `EST. 1752`.
- Full-colour is the default on warm cream/light fields.
- Black is for one-colour reproduction.
- White is for dark backgrounds and can appear blank on white.
- The simplified file is interim only; do not treat it as the final favicon or tiny mark.
- Keep at least 10% of total logo width clear on all sides.
- Prefer at least 120 px wide digitally; never below 96 px without visual QA.
- The trading-post reference image contains legacy operating text. Do not copy its address, hours, descriptor, or other live details without separate verification.
- Do not assign Medicine Wheel teachings or cultural meaning to the colour rail without owner/designated cultural approval.
